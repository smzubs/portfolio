## 👋 About me

I spent **7 years in construction environmental health & safety**, living in OSHA standards, hazard controls, audits, and documentation. That work is fundamentally **governance, risk, and compliance** — identify risk, design controls, prove they hold. I'm now bringing that mindset to **data privacy and software security**, while building and operating a studio of AI products full-stack.

**Why this matters for a GRC / privacy role:**

- I think in **controls and evidence**, not just features — the core of any privacy program.
- Every product I ship uses **least-privilege access, row-level data isolation, and auditability** as defaults, not afterthoughts.
- I can **read and write the technical layer** that privacy and compliance increasingly depend on (access control, data flows, retention, integrations).

**Currently:** pursuing **CIPP/US (IAPP)** · building at Verdorian Technologies LLC · based in Tennessee.
