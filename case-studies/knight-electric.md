## Summary

A corporate website rebuild for an electrical and HVAC contractor — a fast, credible, mobile-first marketing site that matches the quality of their field work and turns visitors into inquiries. Included here as an example of front-end delivery and shipped client work.

## Problem

An established contractor needed a modern web presence. The existing site was dated and didn't reflect a serious, capable company or convert visitors well.

## Solution

A statically-rendered, mobile-first marketing site with clear services, credibility signals, and a direct path to contact — fast on a phone in the field and simple to maintain.

## My Role

**Solo builder** — design and front-end implementation, with an AI-assisted development workflow.

## Development Approach

Built with an AI-assisted workflow using Claude Code / Codex. I owned the layout, responsive behavior, and content structure; AI tools accelerated implementation.

## Key Features

- Mobile-first layouts (designed from 375px up)
- Statically rendered, served from the edge for fast loads
- Clear services, credibility, and contact path

## AI / Automation Features

- Not an AI product — included as a front-end delivery example.

## Tech Stack

- **Frontend:** Next.js, Tailwind CSS
- **Deployment:** Vercel

## Architecture Overview

> Illustrative pattern — a statically-rendered marketing site.

flowchart LR

## Safety / Security / Compliance Notes

No proprietary source or client data is reproduced here. As a static marketing site, there is no database or sensitive data surface.

## Business Value

- Designed to present the company credibly and convert visitors into inquiries
- Designed for near-instant loads on mobile, where the audience finds contractors

## What I Learned

For a marketing site, restraint wins — clear structure and speed matter far more than cleverness.
